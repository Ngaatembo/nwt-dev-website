Frequently asked questions

What is a landing page?

A focused page with one job: getting a visitor from an advert or link to enquire — by form, WhatsApp or phone.

Can it be tracked in Google Ads?

We can set up tracking for form submissions, WhatsApp clicks and call clicks so you can see which enquiries come from a campaign.

How much does a landing page cost?

Landing pages are scoped and quoted individually. The Starter package (from $150) covers up to 5 pages if you also need a small website.
