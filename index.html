About NWT Dev

NWT Dev is a digital solutions company run by Ngaatendwe, building for businesses across Zimbabwe — from restaurant and hospitality sites to logistics and livestock marketplaces.

The focus is on things that keep working after launch: real payments, real bookings, real ongoing maintenance — not a one-time handoff and a developer who disappears once the invoice is paid.

Every project starts from what the business actually needs to run day to day, then gets built to match — not the other way around.
