Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on pricing, timelines, ownership and support.

Pricing & scopeHow do you determine pricing and project scope?

Packages above cover common project shapes. Anything bigger or more specific — a marketplace, a custom platform, deep integrations — is scoped after an initial conversation, with a fixed price and clear deliverables agreed before any work starts. No hidden extras.

Timelines & deliveryHow long does a typical build take from kickoff to launch?

A Starter or Standard site is usually a few days to a week. Business and Pro builds with custom features typically run two to four weeks, depending on scope and how quickly content is supplied.

Architecture & techWhy choose custom engineering over WordPress, Shopify or Wix?

Builder platforms are fine for the simplest sites. Once a business needs real logic — bookings, payments, admin dashboards, marketplaces — templates fight you. We build exactly what the business needs, nothing bolted on.

Support & ownershipDo we own the source code and IP once the build is finished?

Yes — once development fees are paid in full, you own the completed site for your business. NWT Dev keeps ownership only of its own reusable tools and methods, not your content or data.

Architecture & techCan you integrate with our existing tools and regional payment gateways?

Yes — Paynow, EcoCash, WhatsApp, Google Maps and most APIs a Zimbabwean business actually uses are things we've already integrated on live projects.

Support & ownershipWhat does post-launch maintenance and support look like?

An optional $25/month plan covers routine updates, content changes and technical support after launch. Larger systems with live data or admin dashboards are scoped and quoted individually, as with Con Z Connect.
