Frequently asked questions

Can you redesign a site built on another platform?

That’s part of the Discover step: we look at what’s on your current site and agree what stays, what changes and what’s missing before design starts.

Redesigns are quoted after we’ve seen your current site. Our website packages start at $150 — see the pricing section on the homepage for what each includes.
